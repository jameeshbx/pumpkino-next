Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?
No. Every Travel Agency signup gets 7 days of full Growth-tier access automatically. If you don't choose a plan by the end of the trial, your account softly moves to Starter — your data, leads and DMC connections are kept.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime from Settings → Subscription. Changes apply immediately and billing is prorated.
Do DMCs pay to use Pumpkino?
No — DMC/Supplier accounts are free. Subscriptions apply only to Travel Agency accounts sending quote requests and managing bookings.
What happens if I go over my plan's limits?
You'll see an upgrade prompt at the exact point you hit a limit (e.g. adding a 4th team member on Starter) — nothing is deleted or blocked retroactively.
